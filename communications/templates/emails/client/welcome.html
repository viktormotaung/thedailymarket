How Ordering Works

🕢 Orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM

To keep our delivery operation running on schedule, orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM each day.

To keep our delivery operation running on schedule, orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM each day.

This gives our team enough time to prepare your order and organise the day's deliveries. Orders confirmed after the daily cut-off may not be accommodated for the same day's delivery.

Payment

The payment guidelines below apply specifically to cash clients.

Orders under R500

For cash clients, orders under R500 may be paid by cash on delivery, or by another payment arrangement agreed with The Daily Market.

Orders over R500

For cash clients, orders over R500 must be paid by EFT.

Payment must be made immediately after the order is confirmed, and the funds must reflect in The Daily Market's business account by 8:00 AM.

This requirement helps us keep the delivery process efficient and also contributes to the safety of our drivers by reducing the amount of cash that needs to be handled during deliveries.

Account Clients

Clients trading on an approved 3-day or 7-day account are subject to a different set of payment and account terms. The cash-client payment rules above do not apply to these clients.

Your Account Manager will advise you of the applicable terms for your account.

Delivery

Monday to Friday · 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Deliveries take place Monday to Friday between 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM.

On the morning of your delivery, you will receive an SMS when your kitchen essentials leave our depot , normally between 8:00 AM and 8:30 AM.

Please make sure someone is available

Please ensure that someone is present at your kitchen to receive the delivery.

Our delivery schedule is planned around multiple kitchens, and delays at one delivery can negatively affect the schedule for the kitchens that follow.

Having someone ready to receive and check your goods helps us keep deliveries on time and efficient for everyone.

We're here to help your kitchen grow.

At The Daily Market, we don't just want to deliver products to your kitchen. We want to become a reliable trading partner that understands your kitchen, the products you use most and the way your business operates.

As we trade together, our team can better understand your needs and help you make smarter purchasing decisions.
