How Ordering Works

🕢 Orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM

To keep our delivery operation running on schedule, orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM each day.

To keep our delivery operation running on schedule, orders must be placed and confirmed by 7:30 AM each day.

This gives our team enough time to prepare your order and organise the day's deliveries. Orders confirmed after the daily cut-off may not be accommodated for the same day's delivery.

Payment Terms

Payment must be confirmed before delivery. The applicable payment arrangement depends on whether you are trading on standard prepaid terms or have been approved for TradeAssist buying power.

Standard Prepaid Customers

The full invoice amount must be paid, and the funds must reflect in The Daily Market's business account by 8:00 AM on your scheduled delivery day.

Your order will proceed for delivery once the required payment has been confirmed.

An Opportunity to Access TradeAssist Buying Power

You do not start with buying power. Customers begin by trading with The Daily Market on prepaid terms. After two consecutive weeks of trading, your food business may qualify for an assessment for TradeAssist.

Depending on your trading activity and approval, you may qualify for one of the following arrangements:

1. One-in-one-out
You settle one purchase before making the next purchase under the arrangement. This allows you to continue trading while keeping each purchase linked to its settlement.

2. 3-day buying power
You pay the approved upfront portion by 8:00 AM on your scheduled delivery day. The remaining balance is then due within the approved three-day payment term.

TradeAssist is a separate business that partners with The Daily Market. Buying power is subject to assessment, approval and the terms confirmed for your food business.

Delivery

Monday to Friday · 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Deliveries take place Monday to Friday between 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM.

On the morning of your delivery, you will receive an SMS when your kitchen essentials leave our depot , normally between 8:00 AM and 8:30 AM.

Please make sure someone is available

Please ensure that someone is present at your kitchen to receive the delivery.

Our delivery schedule is planned around multiple kitchens, and delays at one delivery can negatively affect the schedule for the kitchens that follow.

Having someone ready to receive and check your goods helps us keep deliveries on time and efficient for everyone.

We're here to help your kitchen grow.

At The Daily Market, we don't just want to deliver products to your kitchen. We want to become a reliable trading partner that understands your kitchen, the products you use most and the way your business operates.

As we trade together, our team can better understand your needs and help you make smarter purchasing decisions.
