Territory Sales Supervisor Opportunity

Help The Daily Market build and grow a sales territory in your area.

About the Opportunity

Startup growth role

About The Daily Market

The Daily Market is a growing online wholesale distribution business supplying food products to traders, vendors, restaurants, caterers, small food businesses and other recurring buyers.

We are still in our early growth stage. This opportunity is best suited for someone who wants to grow with the business, build a territory from the ground up and become part of a long-term commercial growth story.

The Ideal Person
  • Young, energetic and hungry to grow
  • Based in or close to the territory they will supervise
  • Understands local traders, vendors and small businesses
  • Can work from home and in the field without daily supervision
  • Confident speaking to business owners and traders
  • Willing to recruit, guide and manage sales representatives
  • Problem-solver who can work in a startup environment
  • Comfortable with performance-based income

Role & Remuneration

Commission-first structure with growth pathway

Key Responsibilities
  • Supervise and grow an assigned sales territory
  • Recruit, onboard and manage sales representatives
  • Identify traders, vendors and small businesses in the area
  • Ensure reps are prospecting, following up and bringing in clients
  • Help secure recurring clients for The Daily Market
  • Track sales activity, client feedback and territory performance
  • Report weekly on prospects, active clients, orders and challenges
  • Protect The Daily Market pricing, brand and client experience
Remuneration

Important: This is commission-only for the first 3 months until the end of probation.

During probation, the Territory Sales Supervisor earns 30% of the total commission generated in their territory.

After successful probation, a basic salary may be introduced based on performance, scorecard results and territory growth.

Additional Earnings
  • Territory commission share
  • Target-based bonuses
  • Growth incentives for recurring clients
  • Future basic salary after successful probation
