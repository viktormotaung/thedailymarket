Sales Representative

Full Role & Remuneration Spec

Role Purpose

Acquire new clients, manage assigned accounts, and drive repeat orders in the field.

Core Responsibilities
  • Prospect and approach potential clients
  • Convert leads into paying clients
  • Maintain assigned client relationships
  • Drive repeat ordering
  • Follow up on payments

Probation: Commission only
