How We Work

Unlike traditional wholesalers, we source products daily from trusted suppliers across Gauteng. This allows us to offer fresh products, competitive pricing and a wider range of products without carrying unnecessary stock.

Our business is built around planning and efficiency, allowing us to provide dependable next-day deliveries.

Ordering & Deliveries

Please take note of the following important operating procedures.

Delivery Schedule

  • We do not offer same-day delivery.
  • Orders confirmed today are scheduled for next business day delivery.
  • Orders placed on Saturday will be delivered on Monday.

Daily Cut-Off Times

  • Orders must be confirmed by 2:00 PM (14:00).
  • Payment must be completed by 3:00 PM (15:00).
  • Payments received after dispatch planning may result in your order moving to the following delivery day.

To assist with reconciliation, please send your Proof of Payment (POP) after making payment. This helps both your business and ours maintain accurate records.

If payment has not reflected or been confirmed before dispatch, unfortunately we will not be able to dispatch your order.

Placing Orders

Orders can be placed through any of the following channels:

  • Online through your customer portal
  • WhatsApp
  • Your dedicated Product Specialist

Please remember our daily cut-off times when placing orders. For special product requests, sourcing enquiries or bulk orders, please speak directly with your Product Specialist.
