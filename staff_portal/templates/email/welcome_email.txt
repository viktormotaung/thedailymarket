HOW WE WORK
============================================================

We source products daily from trusted suppliers across Gauteng.

Rather than carrying large amounts of stock, we purchase products based on customer demand. This allows us to provide fresh products, competitive pricing and a wider product selection.

Our business is built around planning and efficiency, allowing us to provide dependable next-day deliveries.

============================================================
ORDERING & DELIVERIES
============================================================

IMPORTANT:

• We do NOT offer same-day delivery.
• Orders confirmed today are scheduled for NEXT BUSINESS DAY delivery.
• Orders placed on Saturday will be delivered on Monday.

Daily Cut-Off Times

• Orders must be confirmed by 2:00 PM (14:00).
• Payment must be completed by 3:00 PM (15:00).

Payments received after dispatch planning may result in your order being moved to the following delivery day.

Please send your Proof of Payment (POP) after making payment.

This helps both your business and ours maintain accurate records.

If payment has not reflected or been confirmed before dispatch, unfortunately we will not be able to dispatch your order.
