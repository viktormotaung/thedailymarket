Become a Supplier

Sell Through The Daily Market

We partner with farmers, producers, and wholesalers to bring quality goods to homes, shops, and restaurants — reliably, fairly, and at scale. Join our network and grow with us.

Guaranteed Demand

Steady orders from retail, wholesale, and food-service clients.

Logistics Support

Cold-chain, storage, and last-mile options where needed.

Clear, On-Time Payouts

Transparent pricing and reliable payment cycles.

Why Partner With Us

  • Consistent weekly volumes with room to scale.
  • Quality-first brand that builds trust with buyers.
  • Multiple channels: Retail Express, Wholesale Trade, Grill.
  • Operational support: forecasting, POs, and returns handling.
  • Simple digital onboarding and supplier portal (coming soon).

How It Works

  1. Apply: Share your products, capacity, and location.
  2. Approve: We review quality, certifications, and pricing.
  3. Onboard: Sign terms, set delivery/collection plan.
  4. Supply: Receive POs, deliver on schedule, get paid on time.

What We’re Looking For

  • Reliable weekly capacity (even if small but consistent).
  • Quality control (grading, packaging, temperature where applicable).
  • Basic compliance: invoices, IDs, producer details, certifications.
  • Willingness to meet our specs and delivery windows.

Products We Accept

🐔 Poultry (whole & portions)
🥚 Eggs
🥩 Red/processed meats
🥬 Fresh produce
🧀 Dairy
🧂 Staples & dry goods

If your product isn’t listed, apply anyway — we’ll review.

Pricing & Payouts

  • Pricing: Agreed per product & grade; reviewed quarterly or by market shift.
  • Payouts: Standard weekly/bi-weekly cycles; faster options for approved partners.
  • Deductions: Optional for packaging, cold-store, or logistics if we provide it.
  • Returns: Documented with QA photos; handled per PO terms.

Documents We’ll Request

  • Business/ID details (company reg or sole prop)
  • Bank confirmation letter
  • Food-safety/abattoir certificates (where applicable)
  • Product spec & packaging details
