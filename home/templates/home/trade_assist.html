Trade Assist

Structured trade support for qualifying merchants who trade consistently with The Daily Market.

Trade Assist is an offering by The Daily Market designed to support qualifying merchants in growing their businesses through structured, responsible trade support.

We’re here to help merchants trade consistently, manage cash flow more effectively, and grow with confidence.

What Trade Assist Is
  • A trade-support offering for qualifying merchants
  • Earned through consistent and responsible trading
  • Designed to support business growth
  • Managed and monitored by The Daily Market
What Trade Assist Is Not
  • Not a loan or cash facility
  • Not a guaranteed entitlement
  • Not long-term credit or financing
  • Not available without qualification

Who Can Qualify for Trade Assist

Registered Businesses

Merchants must be CIPC registered and operate as a recognised business entity.

Active Business Banking

An active business bank account is required for verification and settlements.

Verified & Compliant

Merchants must complete required verification and provide any supporting documents requested.

How Trade Assist Works

  1. Register and trade with The Daily Market
  2. Trade consistently using pre-paid purchases
  3. Your weekly trading activity is tracked automatically
  4. Eligibility is earned over time
  1. At 4 consecutive weeks, you may request Trade Assist
  2. At 8 consecutive weeks, qualifying merchants may be activated automatically
  3. Your limit is based on recent average spend
  4. Responsible use supports future increases

Verification & Documentation

To ensure Trade Assist is offered responsibly, we may request:

  • CIPC registration documents
  • Proof of business banking
  • Business contact details
  • Director or authorised representative information
  • Any additional documents required for verification

Trade Assist exists to support merchants who trade responsibly and consistently.
