Why we exist

More than a place to buy food.

We want sourcing stock to feel simpler, more dependable and better suited to the realities of running a food business.

Every day, food businesses do more than prepare meals. They serve neighbourhoods, create livelihoods and bring people together.

The Daily Market exists to support the people behind those businesses. From takeaways and kota shops to restaurants, caterers, retailers and food entrepreneurs, we understand that having the right products at the right time matters.

Our focus is practical: offer a useful range of food products, make pricing easy to understand, provide a straightforward way to browse and order, and build dependable relationships with the businesses we serve.

We are building a market that brings product discovery and food-business supply together. That means a public catalogue where customers can explore products and pricing, supported by a shopping experience designed to make repeat ordering easier.

We are here for the people who make food their business — and the communities they serve.
