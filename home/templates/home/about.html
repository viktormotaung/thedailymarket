About Us

Our Story

The Daily Market was founded with one goal in mind: to make quality food accessible, affordable, and reliable — whether you’re running a household, a restaurant, or a retail business.

The Daily Market started with nothing more than a dream, a Polo, and a love for food. In those early days, the backseat doubled as a warehouse and the dashboard as an office—chickens, veggies, and hopes all packed into one ride. It wasn’t glamorous, but it was real: every stop built trust with a new supplier, every delivery forged a bond with a new client. Those humble beginnings taught us that food is more than just a product; it’s a story of people, families, and communities connecting around a table.

Out of that grind grew a bigger vision—to merge the warmth of food with the power of technology. Today, The is no longer just one man hustling from supplier to client, but a platform and ecosystem designed to scale that same trust and care. We’re building a marketplace where suppliers are empowered, clients get reliability and value, and families never worry about quality. From the Polo days to a growing digital platform, we carry the same spirit: family-driven, supplier-backed, client-focused. And as we grow, we’re not just selling food—we’re building Africa’s daily market of the future, one delivery, one relationship, one story at a time.
