Our story

Building Businesses.

Food supply is where we start. Stronger businesses, trusted relationships and long-term growth are what we are building together.

Why we exist

A commercial partner for food businesses.

The Daily Market is more than a food supplier. We are the commercial company within an ecosystem designed to help responsible food businesses operate, grow and succeed.

Every successful business begins with a vision. Ours began with a simple belief: if food businesses had access to reliable products, professional service and trusted relationships, they would have a stronger foundation to grow.

The Daily Market was founded to become more than another food wholesaler. We wanted to become a dependable commercial partner for restaurants, caterers, retailers, street food traders, spaza shops, takeaways, wholesalers and every entrepreneur serving their community through food. By combining quality products with consistent service, we help businesses focus on what matters most — serving their customers with confidence.

As we worked alongside our Members, we recognised that supplying products alone was not enough. Businesses needed guidance. They needed opportunities. They needed long-term partnerships that extended beyond every purchase. This understanding led to the development of Trade Assist — our Membership ecosystem dedicated to supporting responsible business growth.

Today, The Daily Market and Trade Assist operate together as one connected ecosystem. The Daily Market supplies businesses with the products they need to operate successfully, while Trade Assist helps those businesses grow through Membership, Buying Power, Trade Points, Strategic Partner Benefits and Business Reviews.

Although each organisation has a different responsibility, they share one purpose: Building Businesses.

Every product supplied, every relationship developed and every opportunity created is designed to help responsible food businesses become stronger, more sustainable and better prepared for the future.
