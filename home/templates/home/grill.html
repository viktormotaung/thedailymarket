Grill Experience

Mini-Restaurants. Maximum Flavour.

The Grill Experience is where our love of food meets fun dining. We’re bringing bold flavours, affordable meals, and family-friendly vibes to everyday communities.

Our Signature Flavours
  • The Signature – smokey, savoury, citrus lift
  • Lemon-Herb – bright, garlicky, fresh
  • Peri-Peri – medium heat, full flavour (turn it up if you dare)
  • Sticky BBQ – sweet-smokey crowd-pleaser

From Soweto, with Flavour

In 2025, we fired up our very first Grill Experience in Soweto. A simple idea: serve quality flame-grilled meals at prices that make sense. It worked—locals loved it, families gathered, and word spread fast.

By end of 2026, we’ll be lighting up new locations in Muldersdrift and Bloemfontein. Each mini-restaurant carries the same spirit: fun, flavour, and value.
