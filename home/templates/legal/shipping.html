Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-04
Jurisdiction: Republic of South Africa

This Shipping & Delivery Policy outlines how deliveries are handled by The Daily Market and applies to all orders where delivery services are provided.

1. Delivery Areas

We deliver to selected areas within South Africa. Delivery availability may vary depending on location, order size, and operational capacity.

2. Delivery Timeframes

Delivery timeframes provided at checkout or during order confirmation are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to traffic, weather conditions, supplier availability, or other factors beyond our control.

3. Delivery Fees

Delivery fees, where applicable, will be communicated prior to order confirmation and may vary based on distance, order size, or delivery method.

4. Delivery Process

Customers are responsible for ensuring that delivery details provided are accurate and complete. Deliveries are made to the address supplied at the time of ordering.

5. Failed or Missed Deliveries

If a delivery cannot be completed due to incorrect information, customer unavailability, or access issues, additional charges may apply or the order may be cancelled at our discretion.

6. Risk & Acceptance

Risk in the products passes to the customer upon delivery. Customers are encouraged to inspect products at the time of delivery and report any issues immediately.

7. Perishable Goods

Due to the nature of perishable goods, it is important that customers are available to receive deliveries at the agreed time. The Daily Market cannot be held responsible for product deterioration caused by delays beyond our control.

8. Changes to Delivery Policy

We reserve the right to update or amend this Shipping & Delivery Policy at any time. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.
